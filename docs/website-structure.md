Sections: logo + tagline → one-paragraph positioning → practice-area grid (12 tiles, linked) → partners strip (4 cards, linked) → clients teaser (linked) → contact CTA.

Careers
Copy exists. Decide: mailto: link vs upload form. A CV upload form captures more applicants but introduces file handling, spam and storage of personal data — which is also why the Privacy Policy becomes mandatory.

Essential for a law firm. Must state that site content is not legal advice and that contacting the firm creates no solicitor-client relationship. Also the conventional way firms in this jurisdiction position a website against the professional advertising rules.

Copy review: superlatives
"Leading full-service law firm" and "distinguished reputation" are the most self-promotional lines on the site. Rule 135, Chapter XII of the Pakistan Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Rules 1976 bars advocates from soliciting work by advertisement while permitting professional cards, nameplates and directory listings limited to qualifications. The partners should read the authoritative rule text and decide; peer practice (e.g. RIAA Barker Gillette Pakistan) names no clients and uses Chambers/Legal 500 citations instead.
